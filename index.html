George Kasey

Media Ethics for Human Ecological Recovery

George Kasey's scholarship represents one of the most sustained challenges to institutional suppression of public knowledge in modern media history.

This work demands restoration of global awareness systematically destroyed through centuries of epistemic violence. It calls for recovery from media addiction obfuscation that clouds civic judgment. And it centers the rights of children as a foundational claim upon which all ethical media practice must rest.

— Editorial Assistant, The George Kasey Foundation Inc.
